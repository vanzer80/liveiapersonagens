# MVP 4 — Prompt operacional — SpongeBob thinking V1

Status: **PRONTO PARA GERAÇÃO**.

Imagem mestre obrigatória: `mvp4-spongebob-master-v1-approved.jpeg` no Google Drive oficial do projeto.

Arquivo final esperado após aprovação: `spongebob-thinking-v1.mp4`.

## Objetivo

Gerar o estado visual `thinking`: SpongeBob recebeu uma pergunta/comentário da LIVE e demonstra que está pensando antes de responder. O clipe deve preservar rigidamente a identidade, o cenário, a câmera e a iluminação da imagem mestre. Não deve haver fala nem movimentos de boca que pareçam diálogo.

## Configuração sugerida no Flow/Veo

- usar a imagem mestre aprovada como referência principal;
- se disponível, usar modo `Frames → First + Last` com a mesma imagem mestre no primeiro e no último frame;
- vertical `9:16`;
- alvo de `5–8 s`;
- single continuous shot;
- câmera fixa;
- sem diálogo; desligar áudio se possível.

## Prompt — copiar e colar

Use the uploaded approved SpongeBob master image as the ABSOLUTE and STRICT visual identity reference for this THINKING animation.

Preserve exactly the same SpongeBob identity, face, eyes, blue irises, eyelashes, nose, cheeks, two front teeth, mouth shape, yellow color, sponge-hole pattern, body proportions, arms, legs, white shirt, red tie, brown square pants, socks and black shoes shown in the approved master image.

Preserve exactly the same underwater livestream studio, background composition, colors, objects, object positions, practical lights, depth of field, lighting direction, color temperature and overall rendering style.

CAMERA LOCK:
Vertical 9:16. Straight-on, eye-level locked camera. Keep the same camera height, camera distance, lens perspective, framing, character scale and centered position as the approved master image. No zoom, pan, tilt, dolly, orbit, handheld movement, reframing, cuts or scene transitions.

ACTION — THINKING STATE:
SpongeBob has just received a viewer question during a livestream and pauses briefly to think before answering.

The performance must be subtle, calm and readable as THINKING — not talking, not laughing and not reacting dramatically.

Use this restrained sequence:

1. Begin in the same neutral friendly pose as the approved master image, facing the viewer.
2. After a short neutral beat, make a VERY SMALL thoughtful head tilt to one side.
3. Let the eyes glance slightly upward and off-center for a brief moment, as if considering an answer.
4. Add one subtle natural blink during the thinking moment.
5. Optionally make a tiny restrained hand/arm adjustment close to the body, but do not raise the arm near the face and do not make a large gesture.
6. Hold the thoughtful pose briefly.
7. Return the eyes toward the viewer and bring the head back close to the original neutral position before the clip ends.

FACE AND MOUTH LOCK:
SpongeBob does NOT speak in this clip.

Keep the mouth CLOSED for the entire animation in a small relaxed smile or neutral closed-mouth expression consistent with the master image.

DO NOT open the mouth.
DO NOT show a dark mouth cavity.
DO NOT animate phonemes.
DO NOT move the jaw like speech.
DO NOT laugh.
DO NOT grin broadly.
DO NOT chew.
DO NOT create a surprised open-mouth expression.
DO NOT change the size, spacing or position of the two front teeth.

EYES:
The eye movement is the main thinking cue.
Use only a subtle glance slightly upward/off-center and then back toward camera.
One natural blink is acceptable.
Do not cross the eyes.
Do not roll the eyes dramatically.
Do not wink.
Do not squint dramatically.
Do not create exaggerated comedic eyelid expressions.

HEAD AND BODY:
Use only a very small head tilt and extremely subtle body sway.
Do not rotate the body away from camera.
Do not turn to profile.
Do not walk, jump, dance, wave, point or move toward the camera.
Do not perform a theatrical 'thinking pose'.
Do not place a finger on the chin.
Do not scratch the head.
Do not introduce any prop.

CONTINUITY LOCK:
No identity drift.
No face morphing.
No eye-size changes.
No sponge-hole changes.
No tooth changes.
No clothing drift.
No background drift.
No lighting drift.
No camera drift.
No new props.
No new characters.

LOOP / TRANSITION REQUIREMENT:
The clip does not need to be a perfect endless idle loop, but its first and last poses should remain close to the approved neutral master pose so the scene controller can transition smoothly from `idle → thinking → speaking` or back to `idle`.

If using First + Last Frames, use the SAME approved master image for both first and last frame so the animation begins and resolves into the same visual baseline.
