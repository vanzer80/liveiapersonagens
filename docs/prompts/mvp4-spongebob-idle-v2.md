# MVP 4 — Prompt operacional — SpongeBob idle V2

Status: **PRONTO PARA SEGUNDA TENTATIVA**.

Imagem mestre obrigatória: `mvp4-spongebob-master-v1-approved.jpeg` (Google Drive oficial do projeto).

Origem do ajuste: a primeira tentativa preservou bem identidade, cenário e câmera, mas abriu a boca de forma ampla e apresentou expressão ocular mais marcada. O V2 prioriza neutralidade facial absoluta.

Arquivo final esperado após aprovação: `spongebob-idle-v1.mp4`.

## Prompt — copiar e colar no Flow/Veo

Use the uploaded approved SpongeBob master image as the ABSOLUTE and STRICT visual reference. Animate this exact image with the smallest possible amount of motion. Do not redesign, reinterpret, restyle, reframe or beautify the character or environment.

IDENTITY LOCK:
Preserve exactly the same SpongeBob face, eye size and shape, blue irises, eyelashes, nose, cheeks, two front teeth, yellow color, sponge-hole pattern and positions, body proportions, arms, legs, white shirt, red tie, brown square pants, socks and black shoes shown in the approved master image.

ENVIRONMENT LOCK:
Preserve exactly the same underwater studio, background composition, colors, objects, object positions, practical lights, depth of field and overall atmosphere from the master image.

CAMERA LOCK:
Vertical 9:16. Straight-on, eye-level, locked camera. Keep exactly the same camera height, distance, lens perspective, framing, character scale and centered position as the master image. No zoom, pan, tilt, dolly, orbit, handheld motion, reframing or cuts.

ACTION — ULTRA-SUBTLE IDLE:
SpongeBob simply waits calmly during a livestream while facing the viewer.

Allowed motion only:
- extremely subtle breathing-like body motion;
- one or two quick, natural full blinks during the entire clip;
- tiny eye micro-movements while returning immediately to camera attention;
- barely perceptible relaxed arm/hand micro-movement;
- extremely small posture sway.

FACE AND MOUTH — HARD LOCK:
The mouth must remain CLOSED for the ENTIRE clip in the same small relaxed smile shown in the approved master image.

DO NOT open the mouth at any moment.
DO NOT show the inside of the mouth.
DO NOT expose a dark mouth cavity.
DO NOT create a larger grin.
DO NOT animate speech-like mouth shapes.
DO NOT move the jaw as if speaking, laughing, chewing or reacting.
DO NOT change tooth size, spacing or position.
DO NOT stretch the cheeks.
DO NOT create a laugh, surprise, excitement or reaction expression.

EYES — HARD LOCK:
Keep the same friendly neutral eye shape as the master image.
Only brief natural blinks are allowed.
DO NOT squint.
DO NOT create half-closed expressive eyes.
DO NOT wink.
DO NOT raise or lower the eyelids for comedic expression.
DO NOT exaggerate eyebrow/eyelash movement.

BODY ACTION RESTRICTIONS:
Do not wave, point, jump, dance, walk, turn, lean significantly, raise the arms, move toward the camera, interact with objects or perform any gesture that could look like a reaction.

LOOP REQUIREMENT:
The opening and ending pose must be visually almost identical. Return the face, eyes, mouth, arms, body position and overall composition to the opening neutral pose before the clip ends. Keep SpongeBob in approximately the exact same screen position throughout.

VISUAL CONTINUITY:
No morphing. No identity drift. No costume drift. No background drift. No lighting drift. No camera drift. No new props. No new characters. No changes to sponge holes, eye proportions, teeth, nose, clothing, arm length or leg length.

TECHNICAL TARGET:
Vertical 9:16 video. Single continuous shot. Locked camera. Smooth stable animation. A 6–10 second clip is acceptable for this prototype; consistency is more important than exact duration. No dialogue and no spoken audio. Prefer no meaningful audio; dynamic TTS will be added externally later.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS:
Only one SpongeBob. No Patrick, Squidward, Sandy, Mr. Krabs, Plankton or other characters. No text, subtitles, captions, speech bubbles, logos, watermark, TikTok UI, livestream UI, microphone, headset, costume changes, extra limbs, duplicated hands, malformed fingers, distorted eyes or scene transitions.

PRIORITY ORDER:
1. Preserve the approved master image identity.
2. Keep mouth closed and facial expression neutral for the entire clip.
3. Preserve camera and framing.
4. Preserve background and lighting.
5. Use only tiny idle micro-movements.
6. Make the beginning and ending loop cleanly.

If any requested movement risks changing the face or opening the mouth, OMIT THE MOVEMENT and preserve the master image instead.

FINAL TARGET:
A nearly still SpongeBob idle loop that looks exactly like the approved master image gently came to life through breathing and one or two natural blinks only. He must never look like he is talking, laughing, reacting or preparing to speak.

## Critério de aprovação

- identidade consistente com a imagem mestre;
- boca fechada durante todo o clipe;
- nenhum sorriso aberto ou cavidade da boca visível;
- nenhuma expressão ocular marcada além de piscadas rápidas;
- câmera e enquadramento estáveis;
- cenário e luz sem deriva relevante;
- início e fim próximos o suficiente para loop;
- sem fala ou personagens/objetos novos.
