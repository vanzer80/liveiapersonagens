# MVP 4 — Prompt mestre V1 — Bob Esponja

Status: **candidato para geração da primeira imagem mestre**. Não considerar aprovado até revisão visual do usuário.

## Objetivo

Gerar a imagem mestre vertical que servirá como referência visual fixa para os clipes `spongebob-idle-v1.mp4`, `spongebob-thinking-v1.mp4` e `spongebob-speaking-v1.mp4`.

## Prompt principal — copiar e colar

Create a premium cinematic 3D vertical livestream portrait of SpongeBob SquarePants, using his licensed, immediately recognizable canonical character identity. Preserve the defining visual traits of SpongeBob: bright yellow porous sponge body, large expressive blue eyes, long eyelashes, prominent front teeth, friendly rounded cheeks, thin flexible arms and legs, white short-sleeve shirt, red tie, brown square pants, white socks with colored stripes and polished black shoes. Keep his proportions recognizable and faithful to the licensed character while translating him into a polished high-end cinematic 3D render suitable for a modern interactive livestream.

SpongeBob is alone, centered and facing directly toward the viewer as if he were the host of a livestream. His expression is cheerful, warm, curious and inviting, with a small natural smile and relaxed attentive eyes. He is not speaking in this still image. His mouth is gently closed or only slightly smiling. His pose must be neutral and reusable as the visual master for later idle, thinking and speaking animation clips. Keep both arms naturally relaxed and visible enough to support subtle future gestures.

Scene: a fixed underwater creator/livestream studio inside Bikini Bottom, designed specifically as a clean broadcast set. Use colorful but tasteful underwater details, soft coral-inspired shapes, subtle sea plants, rounded nautical furniture and a few softly glowing practical lights in the background. The environment should feel unmistakably underwater but should not compete with SpongeBob. No other characters. No readable signs. No screens with text. No embedded livestream interface.

Composition: vertical 9:16, optimized for 1080x1920. Medium shot showing SpongeBob clearly from approximately mid-thigh/waist upward, including his shirt, red tie and brown pants, with enough room around his arms for small future gestures. SpongeBob should occupy roughly 55–65% of the vertical frame. Keep moderate clean negative space above and to both sides for future TikTok LIVE overlays such as comments, gifts, ranking and like goals. Keep the face large and clearly readable for later speaking animation and approximate lip-sync.

Camera: locked eye-level camera, straight-on view, centered composition, natural portrait perspective, no wide-angle distortion, no Dutch angle, no tilt, no pan, no zoom, no fisheye. The camera position and framing must be easy to reproduce exactly in future generated clips.

Lighting: premium cinematic 3D lighting with a soft flattering warm key light on SpongeBob's face and front body, subtle cool underwater fill, gentle rim light separating him from the background, soft realistic shadows, polished global illumination, rich but controlled color, realistic material response, subtle volumetric underwater atmosphere and moderate depth of field. SpongeBob must remain sharp and visually dominant while the background is softly blurred.

Rendering style: high-end feature-animation quality 3D, polished physically based materials, detailed but faithful sponge texture, clean edges, expressive eyes, believable soft subsurface light response, cinematic realism applied to a cartoon character without redesigning him into a human or changing his iconic identity. Preserve the classic recognizable color palette and costume. Do not make him grotesque, photorealistic like a real sea sponge, hyper-detailed horror, plush, toy-like, clay, LEGO-like, anime, 2D flat illustration or a different character redesign.

Continuity lock for all future assets: this exact SpongeBob face, eye shape, body proportions, sponge-hole pattern, colors, clothing, studio background, object placement, lighting direction, camera height, camera distance, lens perspective and framing will become the master reference for every MVP 4 SpongeBob clip. Prioritize visual consistency and animation usability over dramatic posing.

IMPORTANT CONSTRAINTS: one SpongeBob only; no Patrick, Squidward, Sandy, Mr. Krabs or any other character; no text; no subtitles; no captions; no logos; no watermark; no UI; no speech bubble; no microphone blocking the face; no headset covering the face; no costume changes; no extra limbs; no duplicated hands; no deformed fingers; no distorted eyes; no asymmetric teeth; no background character silhouettes; no camera movement; no extreme facial expression.

Final result should look like a clean, premium master promotional frame for an interactive SpongeBob AI livestream: instantly recognizable, friendly, colorful, technically stable, easy to animate, and suitable as the strict visual reference for idle, thinking and speaking video states.
